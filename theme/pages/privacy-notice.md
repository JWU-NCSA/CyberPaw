# Privacy Notice

This notice explains what the JWU CyberPaw CTF collects and why.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** username, email address, and a securely hashed password. We never store
  your password in plain text.
- **Optional profile details:** affiliation and website, only if you add them.
- **Competition activity:** challenges you open, flags you submit (right and wrong), hints you
  unlock, solves and scores, with timestamps.
- **Connection information:** IP addresses, kept in logs for security and to enforce the
  [Rules](/rules).

## How we use it

- To run the competition: accounts, scoring and the scoreboard.
- To send account emails, such as email verification and password resets. No marketing emails.
- To keep the competition fair and secure, and to investigate rule violations.

## What others can see

- Your **username, score, solves and badges** are shown on the scoreboard and your public profile.
- Your **email address is never shown** to other players.

## Who has access

Only the CyberPaw organizers and authorized JWU staff. We do not sell or share your data with
anyone else.

## Keeping and deleting data

Competition data may be kept after the event for results and to improve future competitions. To
have your account deleted, contact the organizers.
