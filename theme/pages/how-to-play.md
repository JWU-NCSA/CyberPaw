# How to Play

New to capture the flag? Here's everything you need to get started.

## What is a CTF?

A **capture the flag** (CTF) is a security competition. Each challenge hides a secret piece of text
called a **flag**. You find it by solving a puzzle: cracking a code, digging through a file, reverse
engineering a program, or finding a bug in a website. Submit the flag and you earn points.

You don't need to be an expert. Every category has beginner challenges, and every challenge you
solve teaches you something new.

## Flag format

Every flag on this site looks like this:

```
CyberPaw{s0me_t3xt_h3re}
```

- Submit the **whole flag**, including `CyberPaw{` and the closing `}`.
- Flags are **case-sensitive** unless the challenge says otherwise.
- No spaces before or after the flag.
- If you find text that looks like a flag but uses a different format, it's probably a decoy.

## How scoring works

- Each challenge is worth points. Harder challenges are worth more.
- Some challenges show **↓ decaying points**: they start high and drop as more players solve them,
  so solving early pays off.
- **Hints** may cost points. The cost is shown before you unlock one.
- The **⚠ triangles** show difficulty, from 1 (easy) to 5 (hard).
- The first player to solve a challenge earns the **🐾 First Paw** badge.
- Ties on the scoreboard go to whoever reached the score first.

## Challenge categories

| Category | What you'll do |
|---|---|
| **Crypto** | Break codes and ciphers, from classic shifts to modern mistakes |
| **Forensics** | Dig through files, images and network captures for hidden data |
| **Web** | Find and exploit bugs in websites built for this competition |
| **Rev** | Reverse engineer programs to figure out what they do |
| **Pwn** | Exploit memory bugs in programs to take control of them |
| **Misc** | Everything else: puzzles, OSINT, scripting and more |

## Tips

- **Read the description carefully.** The title and wording often contain hints.
- **Download every file** and look at it in more than one way.
- **Search the web.** Looking things up is part of the game.
- **Keep notes** of what you tried. It saves time when you come back.
- **Stuck?** Try a different challenge and come back later, or unlock a hint.

## Useful free tools

- [CyberChef](https://gchq.github.io/CyberChef/) for decoding and converting data
- [Wireshark](https://www.wireshark.org/) for network captures
- [Ghidra](https://ghidra-sre.org/) for reverse engineering
- Your browser's developer tools (press F12) for web challenges

Before you start, read the [Rules](/rules). Good luck, wildcat! 🐾
